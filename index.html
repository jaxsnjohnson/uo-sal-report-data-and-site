About UO Salary Transparency

Democratizing access to public records.

Why this exists

Public salary reports should be accessible. This independent project makes University of Oregon reports searchable, with original sources attached to every observation.

Data sources and limits

The supplied SharePoint archive contains 93 preserved PDFs and 353,333 imported job records. Extraction was checked on every page; individual rows have not all been manually verified. Verify amounts against their linked source PDFs.

Report rows are not verified headcounts. Nine-month rates, twelve-month rates, and fiscal-year actual pay remain separate.

Methodology

  • Original PDFs are preserved with checksums before import.
  • Pay stays in its reported units; no annualization or FTE multiplication is guessed.
  • Records are linked across reports only through an explicitly reviewed identity key.
  • Years of service cannot be calculated without verified hire dates.
Read the Methodology

Use Advanced to select the report period and pay measure. The report-series selector separates fall census rates, fiscal-year actual pay, and earlier personnel lists. Older fiscal-year personnel lists report rates, not actual earnings.

Changing period or pay measure changes the population being compared. Full-time means a reported appointment FTE of at least 1.0.
