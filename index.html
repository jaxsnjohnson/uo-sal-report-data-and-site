About UO Salary Transparency

Democratizing access to public records.

Why this exists

Public records should be truly public—not just available, but accessible. This project is a civic hacking experiment designed to liberate data from static PDFs and make it searchable, analyzable, and transparent for the entire community.

⚠️ Data Disclaimer

This data is parsed automatically from official UO salary reports, preserved as original PDFs. While we strive for 100% accuracy, the OCR conversion process can occasionally result in errors.

Note: Report coverage and pay definitions vary by period. Salary rates are not actual earnings. Name changes may not be tracked across different reports. Always refer to official documentation for official purposes.

Methodology

  • PDF salary reports are converted to text, then parsed using deterministic rules.
  • Report entries are grouped into dated histories, with original job details and source links preserved.
  • Exact printed names group records for browsing. Shared names and spelling changes can make histories incomplete or ambiguous; these are not verified identities.
  • Salary totals use the reported full-time rate × appointment percent, summed across jobs. Nine- and twelve-month terms stay as reported; no 12/9 or hourly conversion is applied.
  • Fiscal-year actual pay is a separate view. It is summed as reported, without multiplying by an unknown FTE.
  • Source-report classification changes are observations, not verified union exclusions. Missing hire dates remain unavailable; job start dates are not substituted.
  • Repeated identical source blocks remain visible and are flagged; their snapshot pay is excluded from comparisons pending review.
  • Historical gaps are retained. Each job links to its original PDF page.

No usage analytics provider is configured for this UO copy. Search and filtering run in your browser.

Inflation-adjusted views use the CPI-U West region series (All items, not seasonally adjusted; 1982-84=100), published by the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (series CUUR0400SA0). The copied CPI table is sparse: adjustments use the latest prior observation within 24 months, and uncovered dates appear as gaps.

  • Appt Percent: The percentage of a full-time appointment (FTE). E.g., 50% means the employee works half-time (0.5 FTE).
  • Annual Salary Rate: The full-time rate for the reported nine- or twelve-month term. The explorer calculates appointment-adjusted term pay as Salary Rate × Appt Percent; this is not actual earnings.
  • Posn-Suff: These UO reports do not supply persistent job identifiers, so changes between individual job rows in different reports are not inferred.
  • Adj Service Date: Not supplied in these UO reports; left unavailable along with First Hired.
  • Classified: Records printed in UO’s classified personnel reports.
  • Unclassified: Records printed in UO’s unclassified personnel reports; the label does not establish bargaining-unit status.
